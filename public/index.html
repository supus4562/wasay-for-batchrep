Aapki Zubaan

Initiative of Wasay M. Jehangiri

Aapki Zubaan is a simple idea: instead of decisions being made for the batch, they come from the batch. What gets upvoted is what gets priority and what gets priority is what actually gets worked on. It’s your voice that shapes what comes next so speak up!

Editor's Note

Every election season, you hear the same thing “we’ll fix the problems.”

But the truth is most of the time those problems are decided for you, not by you.

This platform is an attempt to change that, even in a small way.
Instead of candidates guessing what matters this space lets the batch speak for itself. You can share issues you actually face and more importantly see what others relate to and care about.

The idea is simple: the problems that rise to the top here should be the ones that get attention not the ones that sound the best in a campaign.

If this works the way it should then it shifts some power back to you. It makes candidates respond instead of decide.
So please use it. Post honestly. Upvote what matters.
So At the end of the day, whoever wins the outcome should reflect your priorities.

Jeet aapki honi chahiye
